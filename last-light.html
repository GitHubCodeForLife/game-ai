THE WAY OF THE WANDERER

Your choices. Your survival.

01 / Fight

All enemies appear together at wave start. Melee enemies form a line; archers fire from behind. Your fighter moves and attacks automatically. Each hit shows actual HP lost.

02 / Adapt

Gain EXP and choose one of three skills. Triggers include hits, critical hits, kills, and wave starts. Click an owned skill to inspect it.

03 / Rest

Heal 25% of maximum HP or gain bonus EXP. Armor absorbs damage before HP. Health, armor, stats, enemy count, and stage progress stay visible on the battlefield; start the next wave there.

04 / Endure

Each stage ends with a different boss. All escorts appear immediately. Defeat every enemy to win, collect gold, and buy upgrades before your next run. Settings has separate music and SFX volume controls.

Space: pause/resume · 1–3: choose a skill · 1×/2×: combat speed.
Switching away pauses combat and mutes audio. Gold, upgrades, and completed stages are saved locally. Reloading ends combat and pays gold for saved completed waves.
