The complete journey

01 / GAME LOOP

A region contains stages. Each stage is one fresh run with its own wave count, difficulty, enemy formation, and final boss.

Introduction→Guided tutorial→Region map→Stage preview→Upgrade / prepare→Fight→Results + gold→Map / shop
01

Inside a run

Fight → gain EXP → choose one of three skills → clear the wave → choose Recover or Train → manually start the next wave.

02

Between runs

Earn gold, buy permanent damage or starting armor, unlock stages, and retry with a new skill combination.

03

Complete the campaign

Clear all six stages to show “Journey complete — 6 / 6.” Every cleared stage remains replayable for normal rewards.

Scope: one fighter, one starting blade, two regions, six stages, three core enemy roles, six boss encounters, the existing 15 skills, persistent gold and upgrades, settings, stage previews, results, and hidden test access. Mid-run resume, optional challenges, extra weapons, accounts, and cloud saves are deferred.

Teach through a short journey

02 / INTRODUCTION

“Your fighter attacks automatically. Choose skills, preserve your health, and defeat the final boss. Every journey earns an opportunity to grow stronger.”

1

Watch the first hit

One practice enemy appears. Explain automatic movement and attacks, enemy HP, floating damage, critical hits, and the separate blue armor bar below the player’s HP.

2

Make a choice

Pause at a guaranteed practice level-up. Show three fixed examples in Condition — Action format, then demonstrate Recover versus Train between practice waves.

3

Prepare for the road

Show the region map, stage difficulty, final boss, expected gold, and the shop. Explain that purchased upgrades persist but run skills reset.

  • First visit offers “Start tutorial” and “Skip to map.” Skipping never blocks the first stage.
  • Practice uses a separate, forgiving two-wave scenario with no permanent rewards, unlocks, purchases, or test-code effects.
  • Tutorial replay is available from Settings outside combat. Replaying preserves real gold, upgrades, clears, and settings.
  • Mark completion only when finished; store dismissal separately so skipping does not repeatedly reopen onboarding.
  • Use touch-friendly Continue, Back, and Skip controls. No keyboard input is required to learn the game.

Keep the battle readable

05 / COMBAT & WAVES
Ready→Deploy every enemy→Brief introduction→Auto-combat ⇄ Skill choice→Wave clear→Rest or final results

All enemies appear immediately

  • Create the full, finite formation at wave start. Include final-wave boss escorts. No delayed reinforcements or mid-wave summons.
  • Frame the arena to show the complete formation. Keep HP labels inside the screen and separate labels when fighters are close.
  • Melee enemies queue without overlapping or charging attacks behind allies. Archers take firing turns.
  • The fighter automatically approaches the nearest living enemy. No manual attack, targeting, or dodge is required.
  • Normal waves use a brief frozen introduction; final waves introduce the boss before combat begins.

HP, armor, and progress

Variable stage length

Each stage defines its own wave list. Total waves = that list’s length; the final wave contains the boss. A five-wave stage has four rest choices; a twelve-wave stage has eleven. Never assume that wave 10 is the universal ending.

During an active wave:
Stage progress = 100 × (completed waves + defeated enemies in current wave ÷ total enemies in current wave) ÷ stage wave count.
Between waves: use completed waves only. Ready before wave 1 = 0%. Victory = 100%. Clamp the displayed value below 100% until victory is confirmed.

Mountain Fortress reference formation

Preserve the current ten-wave formation as one stage: 3 normal → 4 normal + 1 archer → 3 normal + 1 tanker → 4 normal + 1 tanker + 1 archer → 4 normal + 1 tanker + 2 archers → 4 normal + 2 tankers + 1 archer → 5 normal + 2 tankers + 2 archers → 6 normal + 2 tankers + 2 archers → 6 normal + 2 tankers + 3 archers → Iron Warden + 2 normal + 1 archer. Each arrow separates waves, not timed spawn groups.

Rest: choose one reward after each nonfinal wave: Recover restores 25% of maximum HP (rounded up, capped at full); Train grants 30% of the current full EXP threshold (rounded up). Recover is disabled at full HP. Resolve earned levels before the next ready screen. No automatic refill on wave clear; no rest after the final wave.

Offer rules

  • Offer three distinct eligible skills, including owned skills below their rank cap.
  • Include at least one eligible direct damage option whenever any remain: First Strike, Third Beat, Sharpened Edge, Keen Eye, or Quick Hands.
  • Exclude capped skills and unmet prerequisites. Show current → next rank values.
  • First Strike and Overflow Guard have one rank; all other skills have three. If fewer than three are eligible, show those choices; if none are eligible, grant +6 flat damage.
  • Pause combat while selecting; resolve one choice per earned level. No reroll in this version.

Armor and effect order

  • Armor cap = floor(maximum HP ÷ 2). One armor absorbs one damage before HP. It does not regenerate automatically.
  • Show actual armor loss and HP loss separately. Direct armor grants do not heal.
  • Overflow Guard converts only excess healing generated by the same killing blow. Nonlethal healing and rest healing cannot create armor.
  • At 98 HP and 4 armor, rank-1 Second Wind plus Overflow Guard on a kill gives 2 HP, 6 converted armor, and 4 direct armor: result 100 HP and 14 armor.
  • First Strike eligibility belongs to each enemy. Hit counters and pending next-hit bonuses reset at wave start; HP, armor, EXP, and ranks persist within the run.
One attack resolution: determine bonuses → roll critical → apply Guard → reduce enemy HP → show actual damage → resolve On Hit / On Critical Hit → resolve On Enemy Die if killed → apply healing and eligible overflow → award EXP → resolve level-up choices → check wave completion. Secondary healing and armor do not trigger new hits.

Every journey builds an advantage

07 / GOLD & UPGRADES

Gold belongs to the player profile. It is separate from EXP, which only levels the current fighter during a run. Start with 0 gold and no purchased ranks.

DifficultyGold / completed waveVictory bonusFirst-clear bonus

Total reward = completed waves × wave gold + victory bonus if won + first-clear bonus if this is the first real victory.
Bamboo Pass first victory: 5 × 10 + 40 + 30 = 120 gold.
Bamboo Pass replay victory: 5 × 10 + 40 = 90 gold.
Defeat during wave 4 after clearing 3 easy waves: 3 × 10 = 30 gold.
  • Track earned wave gold during the run, then credit the wallet once when the run ends. The boss wave counts as one completed wave.
  • Incomplete waves and individual kills grant no gold; kills still grant normal EXP. Victory requires the player alive and every final-wave enemy defeated.
  • Defeat and explicit abandonment pay completed-wave gold only; no victory bonus, first-clear bonus, or stage unlock.
  • Record the reward and first clear together. Reopening results, retrying, refreshing, or pressing an action twice cannot award the same run again.
  • Replays intentionally earn normal gold. First-clear rewards are once per stage, not once per visit or session.

Apply once before combat

Starting HP = 100. Starting base damage = 18 + 2 × blade rank. Starting armor = min(5 × armor rank, armor cap). Level 1, EXP 0, no run skills, 5% critical chance, 0.85s attack interval.

Run percentage bonuses use this upgraded starting base damage. Example: blade rank 2 gives 22 base damage; Sharpened Edge rank 1 makes 30.8 damage before existing hit rounding, criticals, and Guard.

Make every purchase explicit

Show current rank, next benefit, current gold, and cost. “Buy upgrade” spends gold and adds exactly one rank together. Disable when unaffordable or maxed. Show “Maximum rank” at rank 5.

The shop is available before or between runs. No buying upgrades mid-run; no automatic purchases and no refund system in this version.

Important distinction: permanent armor is a starting shield, not damage reduction and not a per-wave refill. Opening Guard remains a separate run skill. Keep the visible blue armor track even at 0 armor. Prices and reward amounts here are proposals, not validated economy balance.

Behavior rules

  • Enable music at 60% on first launch, starting playback after the first click, tap, or key press. Sound effects start muted at a remembered 80% volume. Preserve returning players’ separate volume and mute choices; unmuting restores the previous nonzero value.
  • Begin audio only after a user gesture. The background score and effects can be muted independently.
  • Opening Settings during combat freezes the simulation. Closing returns to a paused state with an explicit Resume action. An existing skill or rest choice stays intact.
  • While viewing audio settings, allow an explicit short preview sound at the selected volume. Leaving the tab mutes all audio and freezes combat.
  • Reduced motion removes camera shake, particles, ambient bobbing, and rising-number movement. Keep static damage feedback, readable HP changes, and attack warnings. Combat timing does not change.
  • Replay tutorial creates a practice session; exiting it returns to the prior menu. It never clears progress or issues gold.

  • Use a versioned local profile for the browser prototype. The playable game stores this profile locally; no account or cloud sync is required.
  • Save a small active-run summary at stage entry and every completed wave: unique run ID, stage ID, completed waves, enemies defeated, final known build, and earned-wave gold. This is a reward record, not a resumable combat state.
  • Backgrounding without a reload preserves the in-memory run and pauses it. A reload or closed tab cannot resume combat in this version; show “Run interrupted” and settle saved completed-wave gold once.
  • On result settlement, save the wallet, first-clear status, unlocks, final result, and settled run ID together. If saving fails, keep the result visible, explain that it is not saved, and offer Retry save. Never display a successful credit that was not stored.
  • An interrupted unfinished wave earns no wave gold. Recover stored summaries conservatively: only a recorded victory may grant completion bonuses or unlocks.
  • Validate loaded values and version; do not silently overwrite an unreadable profile. Offer a clear recovery message. Clearing browser site data can remove local progress.

Hidden testing access

11 / DEVELOPER DESIGN

Provide the requested TUGAME shortcut in the future game. It is documented here for development, but omitted from normal player-facing help.

Unlimited gold without damaging the real save
  • On map or stage preview, type TUGAME (case-insensitive, consecutive letters within five seconds). Ignore typing inside inputs, textareas, editable content, and modifier-key shortcuts. Clear an incomplete sequence when context changes.
  • On touch devices, tap the Last Light title five times within three seconds on the map to open a hidden code-entry dialog. Enter the same code.
  • Show an infinity symbol in the test wallet and a small “Test mode” indicator. Use a flag rather than saving numeric Infinity as gold.
  • Testing creates a temporary copy of the real profile. Upgrade purchases cost zero but still respect rank caps and starting-stat formulas. Unlimited gold does not mean unlimited damage or armor.
  • Test rewards, free upgrades, and stage clears affect only the temporary copy. Leave real gold, upgrades, first-clear rewards, and unlocks unchanged.
  • Typing the code again on the map exits testing and restores the real profile. Reloading also exits test mode. Disable switching mode during a run; finish or abandon the test run first.
  • Test mode immediately opens every region and stage, including Japan and Egypt, without creating fake clears. Exiting or reloading restores the real profile and its normal unlock rules.
  • Tutorial practice remains separate and cannot activate the code. Result screens in test mode say “Test run — real progress unchanged.”

No hidden shortcut or gold feature is implemented by this HTML document.

Build order

  1. Introduce stage definitions and remove hard-coded ten-wave completion, HUD, boss, and progress checks.
  2. Add profile persistence, reward receipts, permanent upgrades, and a shop.
  3. Add the introduction, practice tutorial, map, and stage preview flow.
  4. Add region environments, themed enemy appearances, and stage-specific bosses.
  5. Add complete results, volume settings, reduced motion, tutorial replay, and isolated testing access.
  6. Playtest all six stages with zero, partial, and maximum upgrades.

Acceptance checks for the eventual implementation

  • A new player can understand the game, skip or complete practice, inspect Bamboo Pass, and start without buying anything.
  • Stages with 5, 6, 8, 10, and 12 waves end at their own final wave. All units appear immediately, including boss escorts; no wave waits for hidden spawns.
  • Armor remains visible beneath HP at 0 and above; starting armor applies once per run and combines correctly with Opening Guard.
  • Purchases spend the correct gold once, persist ranks, respect caps, and improve displayed starting stats. In-run skills reset without removing permanent upgrades.
  • Victory, defeat, abandonment, interruption, repeated result clicks, and reloads each produce the correct single reward receipt.
  • A repeat clear never awards another first-clear bonus. Finishing all six stages shows campaign completion without disabling replays.
  • Independent audio levels, mute memory, system reduced motion, explicit motion preference, and safe tutorial replay work on desktop and touch.
  • TUGAME grants unlimited gold, free capped upgrades, and access to every region and stage in a temporary profile. It does not create fake clears. Exiting or reloading restores real progress and normal stage locks.
  • Check readable enemy labels and usable controls on phone portrait and landscape. No manual dodge is needed to survive any boss pattern.
Still to tune: exact formations for the five new stages, regional enemy stats, final boss timings, economy pacing, and the intended success rate per difficulty. The wave counts, names, costs, reward schedule, and unlock graph in this document are concrete proposals for that work.
